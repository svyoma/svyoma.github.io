Works of darśana, from sūtra manuals to kārikā treatises, are compressed by design and read through their commentaries. Each work here opens adhyāya by adhyāya. The sūtra comes first, and the vṛtti or ṭīkā sits beneath it, ready when you want it.

Faithful numberingSūtras keep the edition's numbers. Wherever the print skips or repeats a number, the reader says so.
